    📜 How to Play

    Goal: clear all 52 cards of the dungeon before losing your 20 lives.

    Cards

    • ♠ Spades / ♣ Clubs — Monsters: deal damage equal to their value (J=11, Q=12, K=13, A=14). Fight them with your weapon (reduced damage) or barehanded (full damage). Monsters fought with a weapon stack on it; barehanded ones go straight to the graveyard.
    • ♥ Hearts — Potions: heal that many lives, up to 20, then go to the graveyard.
    • ♦ Diamonds — Weapons: equip one at a time. It reduces damage from monsters, but cannot block a monster worth more than the last one it defeated. Durability shows the highest monster it can block (∞ = any). Equipping a new weapon sends the old one and its stack to the graveyard.

    Rooms

    • A room has 4 cards. Interact with 3 of them to clear it; the untouched card carries into the next room and 3 new cards are dealt.
    • Skip Room returns all 4 cards to the bottom of the deck (right to left). It can't be used twice in a row or after acting this turn.

    Scoring

    • Death: negative sum of monster values left in the deck and room.
    • Victory: lives remaining. A perfect 20-life win adds the value of the strongest potion left in the final room.
